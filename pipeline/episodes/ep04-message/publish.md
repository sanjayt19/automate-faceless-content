## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** The Cold Message That Actually Gets Answered #shorts

**Description:**
The template outreach message is dead. Inboxes are full of fluent generated text,
recruiters and managers learned the shape of it, and they delete on line one.

What still works is the part a machine cannot fake. Six lines, no attachment. Line
one says who you are in two lines, not a biography. Line two proves you looked, and
it has to name something only a reader of their actual work would know, not the
company mission statement. Line three asks for fifteen minutes about the work, never
for a job, and offers two specific slots so saying yes is one word.

Most people will not reply. Follow up once, a week later, shorter than the first
message. Then stop.

Ten specific notes beat two hundred generic ones, because specific cannot be mass
produced.
