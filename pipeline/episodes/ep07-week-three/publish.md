## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Why Your Job Search Dies In Week Three #shorts

**Description:**
Week one you message ten people. Week two four of them reply. Week three you have
lost track of who owes you what, and the whole thing quietly stops. That is not a
motivation problem, it is a tracking problem.

The fix is a fixed number. Keep a small set of live conversations, three is plenty,
and never let the set shrink. Each one needs real follow up: notes, a thank you, a
next step. More than three and you start dropping threads, and dropped threads do
not come back.

Then pick one cadence and hold it. Same day, same hour, every week, not whenever you
feel like it. On that day ask three questions. Who owes me a reply. What did I
promise. Who has gone quiet for two weeks. Two quiet weeks means closed, so close it
without resentment and refill the slot the same day.

One out, one in. The set never runs empty, so the search never stalls.
