# Forty Companies In Forty Minutes

Book: The 2-Hour Job Search by Steve Dalton. Episode 3 of 5. 40 beats, 208 words, about 85 seconds.

## Narration, as spoken

Applying less only works with aim. So you need a list. Steve Dalton calls it LAMP. Four letters. Four columns. One afternoon. Forty employers. Start with the list itself. Set a timer for ten minutes. Name every employer you can. Do not judge them yet. Judging is what stops you. Then do that four times. Forty names in forty minutes. Now add the second column. Advocacy. Who could speak for you? Someone inside who knows your work. Dalton changed this rule himself. He used to say alumni first. Now he says relevance first. Target people holding that job. A shared school helps less. Third column is your motivation. Would you actually go there? Be honest on this one. Half the list will fail it. Fourth column. Any open posting. This one counts the least. A posting is a weak signal. One in five is fake. Advocacy and motivation carry the rank. The posting only breaks ties. Now sort by that score. Work the top five first. Depth beats breadth every time. Five conversations beat two hundred applications. That is the whole trade. And the list does not expire. Postings vanish. Employers stay. Reuse it for the next search. Forty minutes buys you aim. Everything after that is easier.
