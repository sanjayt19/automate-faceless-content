## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Build Your Job Search Target List In 40 Minutes #shorts

**Description:**
Applying less only helps if you aim. Steve Dalton's method builds the aim first:
four ten minute bursts to name forty employers, without judging any of them while
you list.

Then three questions per employer. Is there someone inside who could speak for you.
Would you actually go there. Is anything open. The first two carry the ranking. The
posting only breaks ties, because roughly one in five postings is a ghost job.

Sort, then work the top five. Five real conversations beat two hundred applications,
and unlike a posting, the list does not expire.

One correction to the original method: Dalton has since dropped alumni-first in
favour of targeting people who hold the job you want.
