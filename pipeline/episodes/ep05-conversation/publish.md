## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** The 15 Minute Call That Changes A Job Search #shorts

**Description:**
They said yes to fifteen minutes. Most people waste it by pitching themselves, which
is the one thing nobody on that call asked for.

Steve Dalton's ladder is five questions in a fixed order, easiest first, most
personal last. Trends: what is changing in this work. Insights: what surprised you
here. Advice: what would you change. Resources: what should I read. Assignments:
what actually fills your week, which is the real job description rather than the
posting.

Take notes and let them talk. Near the end there is exactly one ask, and it is not
for a job or a referral: who else should I meet. Referrals get offered, never
requested.

Send thanks the same day. Then wait a week and write back saying what you did with
their advice. Almost nobody does this, which is exactly why it is remembered.

One conversation becomes three.
