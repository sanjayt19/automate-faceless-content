# The Fifteen Minutes That Change Everything

Source: *The 2-Hour Job Search* by Steve Dalton. Own words only, no quoted passages.

## Narration

They said yes, now what? Most people waste it. You have fifteen minutes, spend twelve of them listening. Do not pitch yourself, nobody asked for that. Dalton hands you a ladder, five questions, strict order. You start with trends, what is changing here? Everyone can answer that, it costs them nothing. Then ask for insights, what surprised you here? Now it is personal. Then you ask advice, what would you change? People love giving advice. Then you ask resources, what should I read? Cheap for them, useful. Assignments come last, on purpose, what fills your week? That is the real job. Trends, insights, advice, resources, assignments, easy first, personal last. Take notes while they talk, do not interrupt to agree. Near the end, one ask, who else should I meet? That is the only ask. Never request a referral, referrals are offered, not requested. Send thanks the same day, three lines, one specific thing. Then wait a full week, write back what you did. Almost nobody ever does this. One conversation becomes three, that is the whole engine.
