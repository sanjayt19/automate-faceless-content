## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Twenty Messages, Zero Replies. Here Is Why #shorts

**Description:**
When nobody answers, most people rewrite the resume. The resume was never read.

Steve Dalton splits the people you contact into three kinds. Boosters answer fast,
warm and specific, and will open doors you did not ask them to open. Obligates answer
eventually, politely and briefly, and give you facts but never advocacy. Curmudgeons
never answer at all, and it is almost never about you.

The hard part is that you cannot tell which is which in advance. Seniority does not
predict it. A shared school does not predict it. Warmth is a personality trait, not a
status you can earn.

So stop trying to pick boosters and start finding them. Contact several people at
once and let the replies do the sorting. Whoever answers warmest gets your time.
Everyone else gets one follow up, then you let them go.

You only need two or three boosters, and boosters introduce you to more boosters.
Volume is not the point. Sorting speed is.
