# Why Your Goals Keep Failing

Book: Atomic Habits by James Clear. Runtime 83 seconds. 40 beats, 202 words.

## Narration, as spoken

Two people join the same gym. Same goal. Same January. By March one still goes. The other quit in week two. The difference was never willpower. James Clear calls it a systems problem. A goal names only the destination. Then it leaves you alone. A system is what Tuesday looks like. Tired, and doing it anyway. Every runner wants to win. So goals cannot separate them. What separates them is the first move. How small it is. How close it sits. Clear's rule is shrink the habit. Shrink until it feels embarrassing. Two minutes of reading. One page. One push up. One song of practice. Two minutes changes nothing physically. That is not the point. It proves you showed up. Identity first. Results follow. Then Clear stacks the deck. Running shoes beside the door. Phone left in another room. Fruit visible. Biscuits hidden. The good choice becomes easy. Most discipline is really arrangement. A room built to help. Willpower is a poor daily strategy. It runs out by evening. Environment never gets tired. So stop setting bigger goals. They were never the problem. Take a smaller step instead. Build a better room. That is Atomic Habits, James Clear. Two minutes. Start tonight.
