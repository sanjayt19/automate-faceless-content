# Episode 8 — Your Resume Gets Seven Seconds

Opens the hiring-side arc. First episode produced in visual format I, the
Apple-advertisement look: seamless white void, one object, studio light.

Source: eye-tracking research on recruiter resume screening, which put the
average first pass at roughly seven seconds. Our own words throughout.

Target 85 seconds. 37 beats, 176 words.

---

Your resume gets seven seconds. Not seven minutes, seven seconds.

Recruiters were tracked with eye cameras. Here is where they look. Your name,
your current title, your company, your dates, the previous title and dates,
then the education line. That is the whole scan. Everything else gets skipped.

Your bullets are not read, your summary is not read, not on the first pass.

So the top third decides, and most people bury it under a skills wall, an
objective, a driven paragraph. None of that survives.

So fix three things. One, match the posting's title, their words, not yours.
Two, add one real number, scale beats adjectives every time. Three, delete the
objective. Nobody has ever read one.

Then test it yourself, cover everything below the fold, and look for seven
seconds. What did you learn? If nothing, rewrite the top.

This is not about design. Fonts never failed anyone, buried information fails
everyone. The scan is shallow and fast, so build for the scan.

Seven seconds is the budget. Spend all of it up top.
