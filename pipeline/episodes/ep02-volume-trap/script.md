# Why Applying To More Jobs Makes It Worse

Book: The 2-Hour Job Search by Steve Dalton. Episode 2 of 5. 40 beats, 197 words, about 85 seconds.

## Narration, as spoken

You applied to two hundred jobs. And heard almost nothing back. It was not your resume. It was the maths. Applying used to cost effort. Now it costs one click. So everyone clicks far more. Every minute, eleven thousand applications. On one platform alone. That is up nearly half. In a single year. Recruiters cannot read that pile. So they lean on filters. And the overflow gets ghosted. Which teaches you to apply more. Which buries them further. That loop has a name. It is a doom loop. Now look at the odds. A cold application converts rarely. Roughly one in a hundred. A referral converts far better. Closer to one in three. Referrals are a tiny share. Of everything companies receive. Yet they fill most roles. So volume is the wrong lever. It is the crowded one. There is a worse problem. Some postings are not real. Roughly one in five. No one is being hired. You cannot see which. Steve Dalton saw this early. In The Two Hour Job Search. Stop chasing every open posting. Pick a short list instead. Then talk to actual people. That channel is not flooded. Fewer applications. Far better odds.
