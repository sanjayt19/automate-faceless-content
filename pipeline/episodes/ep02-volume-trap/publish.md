## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Why Applying To More Jobs Makes It Worse #shorts

**Description:**
Applying used to cost effort. Now it costs one click, so everyone clicks more.
LinkedIn takes around eleven thousand applications a minute, up roughly 45% in a
year. Recruiters lean harder on filters and ghost the overflow, which teaches
candidates to apply even more.

A cold application converts at roughly one percent. A referral converts closer to
one in three. Referrals are a small share of applications and a large share of
hires. And about one in five postings is a ghost job.

The fix is the one Steve Dalton argued for in The 2-Hour Job Search: stop chasing
every open posting, shortlist, and talk to people.
