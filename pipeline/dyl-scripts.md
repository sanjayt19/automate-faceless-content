# Designing Your Life — twelve scripts

Bill Burnett and Dave Evans. Our own words throughout, no quoted passages.

Every one of these stands alone. No callbacks, no part numbers, nothing that
needs another reel to make sense. Each names a problem an active job seeker
already has, explains one mechanism, and hands over one thing to do.

The hook is beat one and it has to land inside three seconds, because it is also
the first burned caption a silent viewer reads.
