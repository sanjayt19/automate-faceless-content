## Episode 1 — Why Your Goals Keep Failing

**Caption**

Two people join the same gym in January. By March only one is still going.

The difference was never willpower. It was the size of the first step and how close
it sat to hand.

Atomic Habits, James Clear. Two minutes. Start tonight.

## Episode 2 — Why Applying To More Jobs Makes It Worse

**Caption**

You applied to two hundred jobs and heard nothing. It was not your resume. It was
the maths.

Applying is free now, so everyone does more of it. The pile grew, the filters got
harder, and the replies stopped.

Fewer applications. Better odds.

## Episode 3 — Forty Companies In Forty Minutes

**Caption**

Forty employers in forty minutes. Four bursts of ten, and you do not judge any of
them while you are listing. Judging is what stops you.

Then rank by who could speak for you and whether you would actually go. The job
posting counts least, because one in five is not real.

Work the top five. Depth beats breadth.

## Episode 4 — The Message That Actually Gets Answered

**Caption**

Your cold message is not being read. It is being pattern matched.

Six lines. No pitch, no resume, no attachment. One line that proves you actually
looked at their work, and an ask for fifteen minutes instead of a job.

Ten specific notes beat two hundred generic ones. Specific is the one thing that
cannot be mass produced.

## Episode 5 — The Fifteen Minutes That Change Everything

**Caption**

They gave you fifteen minutes. Spend twelve of them listening.

Five questions, in order: trends, insights, advice, resources, assignments. Easy
first, personal last.

One ask at the end, and it is never for a job. Who else should I meet.

Then a week later, write back and tell them what you did with their advice. Almost
nobody does this.

## Episode 6 — Twenty Messages, Zero Replies

**Caption**

Twenty messages, zero replies. Stop rewriting your resume. It was never read.

People come in three kinds: the ones who answer warmly, the ones who answer out of
duty, and the ones who never answer. You cannot tell which is which in advance.

So contact several at once and let the replies sort them. Two or three warm ones
carry an entire search.

## Episode 7 — Why Your Job Search Dies In Week Three

**Caption**

Job searches do not die from low effort. They die in week three, when you lose track
of who owes you what.

Keep three live conversations. Never let the set shrink. Check the whole set on the
same day every week.

Two quiet weeks means closed. Close it, refill the slot, same day.

One out, one in.
