# Pilot slate: episodes 1 to 5

Format: non-fiction book summary. Illustrated stills with camera motion. 85 seconds.
Vertical 9:16, burned captions, cover frame.

Each episode is one book reduced to one mechanism the viewer can act on. Not a
chapter-by-chapter recap. The test is whether a viewer can repeat the idea to
someone else after one watch.

| # | Book | Author | The one mechanism | Hook angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Atomic Habits | James Clear | Behaviour follows environment, not willpower | The reason your goals fail is that you set goals |
| 2 | The Psychology of Money | Morgan Housel | Wealth is what you do not spend, so it is invisible | The richest person in the room looks poor |
| 3 | Deep Work | Cal Newport | Attention residue makes switching cost real hours | You have not worked a full hour in years |
| 4 | Thinking, Fast and Slow | Daniel Kahneman | Two systems, one of them is lying to you confidently | Your brain has a fast mode that is usually wrong |
| 5 | Sapiens | Yuval Noah Harari | Shared fictions are what let strangers cooperate | Money is a story we all agreed to believe |

Episode 1 is the calibration run. It sets the style preset, the narrator voice, the
caption treatment, and the cover template. Episodes 2 to 5 inherit all four and are
therefore much faster.

Swap any title before production starts. After episode 1 the look is locked.
